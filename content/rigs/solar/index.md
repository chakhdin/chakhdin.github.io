The Sun is imaged with the [Bresser 127L](/equipment/bresser-127l/) refractor and the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/), in two ways.

- **Telescope:** Bresser Messier AR-127L, 127 mm, 1200 mm at f/9.4
- **White light:** through a Tecnosky Herschel wedge
- **H-alpha:** a [Daystar Quark Chromosphere](/equipment/daystar-quark-chromosphere/) double-stacked with the etalon of the [Lunt LS35THa](/equipment/lunt-ls35tha-b600/)
- **Camera:** [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/), monochrome with a global shutter
- **Mount:** [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/) with the Rowan belt modification
