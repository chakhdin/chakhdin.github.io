The wide-field rig is built around the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/) on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/).

- **Telescope:** Takahashi FSQ-85EDX, 85 mm, 450 mm at f/5.3
  - with the Takahashi 1.01× flattener: about 455 mm
  - with the [Takahashi QB 0.73× reducer](/equipment/takahashi-reducer-qb-0.73x/): about 330 mm at f/3.9
- **Camera:** [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/), cooled monochrome APS-C
- **Filters:** ZWO EFW 7 × 36 mm filter wheel with [Antlia LRGB-V Pro](/equipment/antlia-lrgb-v-pro-36mm-filters/) and [Antlia 3 nm Pro SHO](/equipment/antlia-pro-3nm-sho-36mm-filters/)
- **Guiding:** ZWO off-axis guider with a ZWO ASI290MM Mini
- **Power and USB:** Pegasus Astro Pocket Powerbox Advance Gen2
- **Mount:** [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/) with the Rowan belt modification

With the ASI2600MM Pro the field is about 2.96° × 1.98° at 1.70″ per pixel with the flattener, and about 4.08° × 2.73° at 2.35″ per pixel with the reducer.
