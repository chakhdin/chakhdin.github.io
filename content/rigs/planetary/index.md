For the planets the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) works at long focal length with a fast planetary camera.

- **Telescope:** Meade 8" ACF, 203 mm, with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/): about 4000 mm at f/20
- **Camera:** [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/), monochrome with a global shutter
- **Colour camera:** [ASI224MC](/equipment/asi224mc/), colour with a rolling shutter (Sony IMX224), for one-shot colour imaging
- **Filters:** ZWO EFW Mini 5 × 1.25" filter wheel with [Optolong LRGB filters](/equipment/optolong-lrgb-filters/) and an IR-pass filter
- **Atmospheric dispersion corrector:** ZWO 1.25" ADC, two adjustable prisms that cancel the colour fringing the atmosphere adds to planets low on the horizon
- **Mount:** [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/) with the Rowan belt modification
