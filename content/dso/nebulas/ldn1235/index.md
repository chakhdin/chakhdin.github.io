This deep exposure captures LDN 1235, famously known as the Shark Nebula, located in the constellation Cepheus. This large, dark molecular cloud complex is part of a vast region of galactic cirrus—faint, dusty material illuminated by the integrated starlight of the Milky Way. Its dense, light-absorbing dust creates the distinct shape of a shark's head and body seemingly cruising through the cosmic ocean.

Accompanying the "shark" are two beautiful blue reflection nebulae, vdB 149 and vdB 150, which are illuminated by nearby hot stars and perfectly highlight the edges of the dark dust. Adding further depth to the background, a distant spiral galaxy (PGC 67671) is visible to the right of the main nebula.
