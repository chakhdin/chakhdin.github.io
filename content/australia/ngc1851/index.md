NGC 1851 is a globular cluster in the constellation Columba, about 40,000 light-years from us. Its core is very compact, with a halo of stars extending well beyond it, and the long focal length resolves the outer regions into individual stars.

Only red, green and blue data was used, so the colours of the stars come directly from the three filters.

The data was captured remotely from Swan Reach, South Australia, on a rig operated with NINA at Swan Reach Imaging (https://swanreachimaging.com.au), and was collected jointly by the group sharing the rig. Stacked and processed in PixInsight.
