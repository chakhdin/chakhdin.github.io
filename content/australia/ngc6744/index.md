NGC 6744 is a large barred spiral galaxy in the constellation Pavo, about 30 million light-years away. Its size and structure, with a bright core, an elongated bar and extended, branching arms, make it similar to what the Milky Way would look like from outside.

The outer arms are faint and need a long integration to show; a smaller companion galaxy lies close to the main disc.

The data was captured remotely from Swan Reach, South Australia, over six nights, on a rig operated with NINA at Swan Reach Imaging (https://swanreachimaging.com.au), and was collected jointly by the group sharing the rig. Stacked and processed in PixInsight.
