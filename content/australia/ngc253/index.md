NGC 253, the Sculptor Galaxy, is one of the brightest galaxies outside the Local Group, about 11 million light-years away. We see it nearly edge-on, which makes its dust lanes stand out against the bright disc. It is a starburst galaxy, forming stars at a much higher rate than the Milky Way.

Mottled dust, pink star-forming regions and the yellow central bar are visible in this image, and the H-alpha data adds detail to the star-forming regions.

The data was captured remotely from Swan Reach, South Australia, over five nights, on a rig operated with NINA at Swan Reach Imaging (https://swanreachimaging.com.au), and was collected jointly by the group sharing the rig. Stacked and processed in PixInsight.
