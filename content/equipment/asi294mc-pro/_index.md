The ZWO ASI294MC Pro is a cooled one-shot colour camera with the Sony IMX294 4/3" sensor: 4144 × 2822 pixels (11.7 MP) of 4.63 µm, with a 14-bit ADC.

Before the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) it was my deep-sky camera, on the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/), the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) and the Skywatcher 150PDS. Today I use it occasionally, when a target calls for a colour camera.
