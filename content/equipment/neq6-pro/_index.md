title: "NEQ6 Pro"
description: "Sky-Watcher NEQ6 Pro: the German equatorial GoTo mount that carries every setup used from Italy."
---
The Sky-Watcher NEQ6 Pro is a computerised German equatorial mount with SynScan GoTo control. It carries all of my Italian setups: the deep-sky refractor and the 8-inch ACF, the planetary train and the solar refractor.

Mine has the Rowan Astronomy belt modification, which replaces the factory spur-gear drive with timing belts to reduce backlash and periodic error. The mount was then tuned by Davide Dal Prato of the Torre del Sole astronomical park in Brembate di Sopra (BG), Italy.
