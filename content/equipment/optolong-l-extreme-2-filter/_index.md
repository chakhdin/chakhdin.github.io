The Optolong L-eXtreme is a dual-band filter with two 7 nm passbands, on H-alpha (656.3 nm) and OIII (500.7 nm). It blocks the rest of the spectrum, H-beta and the lines of artificial lighting included, so a colour camera can record emission nebulae under a bright sky. I used it with the [ASI294MC Pro](/equipment/asi294mc-pro/) on the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/).
