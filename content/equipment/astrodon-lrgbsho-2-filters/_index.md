The Swan Reach remote rig uses Astrodon filters, now sold under the CXB name: the Gen 2 E-series Tru-Balance LRGB set and 3 nm Ha, OIII and SII narrowband filters, in a ZWO EFW 7 × 2" filter wheel.

The E-series is designed for roughly equal 1:1:1 colour combine weights with full-frame sensors, and the maker recommends it for back-illuminated and Sony sensors. A spectral gap between the G and R filters reduces the light of high-pressure sodium street lamps, and B and G both pass over 97 % of the O III line. The red filter is narrower than an orange one, which raises the contrast of H II regions in galaxies. The filters are 3 mm fused silica and parfocal with the Astrodon narrowband filters.

They work with the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) on the [RCOS 16"](/equipment/rcos-16/) of the [Swan Reach remote rig](/rigs/swan-reach/).
