---
The Optolong L-Pro is a broadband light-pollution filter. It passes the main nebula lines (H-beta, OIII, H-alpha, NII and SII) and most of the continuum, but suppresses the mercury (435 and 578 nm) and sodium (589 nm) lines of street lighting and blocks infrared. Because it keeps most of the continuum, it also suits galaxies and star clusters. I used it on the [Skywatcher 150PDS](/equipment/skywatcher-150pds/) for M13, M22 and M45.
