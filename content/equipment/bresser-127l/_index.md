The Bresser Messier AR-127L is a 127 mm achromatic refractor with 1200 mm focal length (f/9.4). It is my solar telescope, on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/) with the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/):

- **White light:** through a Tecnosky Herschel wedge.
- **H-alpha:** with a [Daystar Quark Chromosphere](/equipment/daystar-quark-chromosphere/) double-stacked with the [etalon of a Lunt LS35](/equipment/lunt35-etalon-double-stack/).
