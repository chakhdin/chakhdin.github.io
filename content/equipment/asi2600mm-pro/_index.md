The ZWO ASI2600MM Pro is a cooled monochrome camera built around the Sony IMX571 back-illuminated APS-C sensor: 23.5 × 15.7 mm, 6248 × 4176 pixels (26 MP) of 3.76 µm, with a 16-bit ADC.

It is my main deep-sky camera, used on two setups that share the same imaging train: the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/) and the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/), both on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/). The train:

- ZWO EFW 7 × 36 mm filter wheel with [Antlia LRGB-V Pro](/equipment/antlia-lrgb-v-pro-36mm-filters/) and [Antlia 3 nm Pro SHO](/equipment/antlia-pro-3nm-sho-36mm-filters/) filters
- ZWO off-axis guider with a ZWO ASI290MM Mini guide camera (Sony IMX290, 2.9 µm pixels)
- Pegasus Astro Pocket Powerbox Advance Gen2 for power, dew heaters and USB

Earlier images with this camera used [Baader LRGB](/equipment/baader-lrgb-36mm-filters/) and [Baader Ha 7 nm](/equipment/baader-ha-7nm-36mm-filter/) 36 mm filters.

A second ASI2600MM Pro, belonging to the rig, works at Swan Reach, South Australia, on the [RCOS 16"](/equipment/rcos-16/) and the [Paramount ME](/equipment/paramount-me/) of the [Swan Reach remote rig](/rigs/swan-reach/). Its train: a ZWO EFW 7 × 2" filter wheel with [Astrodon LRGBSHO 2" filters](/equipment/astrodon-lrgbsho-2-filters/), an off-axis guider with a ZWO ASI174MM Mini guide camera, and a Pegasus Astro Pocket Powerbox Advance Gen2.
