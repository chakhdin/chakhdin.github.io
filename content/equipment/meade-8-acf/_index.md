The Meade 8" ACF is a 203 mm catadioptric telescope with Meade's Advanced Coma-Free optics: about 2000 mm focal length at f/10. It works on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/) in two roles.

- **Planetary:** with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/) (about 4000 mm at f/20), the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/) and a ZWO EFW Mini 5 × 1.25" filter wheel holding [Optolong LRGB filters](/equipment/optolong-lrgb-filters/) and an IR-pass filter.
- **Deep sky:** my second main setup, with the same [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) imaging train as the Takahashi. Earlier deep-sky images were taken with the colour [ASI294MC Pro](/equipment/asi294mc-pro/).
