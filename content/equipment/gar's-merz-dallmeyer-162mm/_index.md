The historic refractor of the Gruppo Astrofili Rozzano (GAR), in the 7 m dome of the Civico Osservatorio Astronomico di Rozzano, inaugurated in May 2023. The 162 mm objective is signed G.&S. Merz in Munich; the mount and the finder are signed J.H. Dallmeyer, London. GAR dates the instrument to the late 19th century, around 1870. It was found abandoned in a private home in Vigevano, has come down to us without structural changes, was functionally restored by ARASS Brera and is protected by the Soprintendenza in Milan.

I used it with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/) and the colour [ASI224MC](/equipment/asi224mc/) for an image of [Jupiter in September 2023](/sso/jupiter/2023-09-09/).
