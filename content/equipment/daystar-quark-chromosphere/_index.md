title: "Daystar Quark Chromosphere"
description: "Daystar Quark Chromosphere: H-alpha filter with a built-in 4.2× telecentric Barlow, tuned for detail on the solar chromosphere."
---
The DayStar Quark is an H-alpha filter in eyepiece form: an etalon with a very narrow passband (DayStar specifies 0.25–0.5 Å) and a built-in telecentric Barlow. The Chromosphere grade is meant for detail on the solar disk rather than prominences. On the [Bresser 127L](/equipment/bresser-127l/) with the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/), I double-stack it with the etalon of a [Lunt LS35THa](/equipment/lunt-ls35tha-b600/) for more contrast.
