A Meade Maksutov-Cassegrain with a 127 mm aperture and a 1900 mm focal length (f/15). The long focal length and the closed tube suit the Moon and the planets. It was my planetary telescope until November 2024, with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/) and the [ASI224MC](/equipment/asi224mc/), before the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) took over.
