The RCOS 16" is a 16-inch (0.41 m) Ritchey-Chrétien telescope by RC Optical Systems, with a carbon-fibre truss and Astro-Sitall mirrors. With the ASI2600MM Pro, its focal length measures 3660 mm on the plate-solved images.

It is the main telescope of the [Swan Reach remote rig](/rigs/swan-reach/) in South Australia, under a roll-off roof at Swan Reach Imaging. It rides on a [Paramount ME](/equipment/paramount-me/) and images with an [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) through [Astrodon LRGBSHO 2" filters](/equipment/astrodon-lrgbsho-2-filters/). A PrimaLuceLab rotator frames the field, a PrimaLuceLab focuser moves the secondary mirror, and dew heaters and fans keep the primary mirror at ambient temperature.

The rig is shared by a small group, so these images are joint work.
