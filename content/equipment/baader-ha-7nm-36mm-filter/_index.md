A narrowband filter with a 7 nm passband on the hydrogen-alpha line at 656.3 nm, the deep red light of ionised hydrogen in emission nebulae. I use the 36 mm version in the filter wheel of the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) on the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/), combined with the [Baader LRGB filters](/equipment/baader-lrgb-36mm-filters/) for natural star colours.
