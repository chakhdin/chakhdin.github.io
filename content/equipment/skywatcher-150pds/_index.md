The Sky-Watcher Explorer 150PDS is a Newtonian reflector with a 150 mm parabolic mirror, 750 mm focal length at f/5, and a 2" dual-speed Crayford focuser (10:1). I imaged M45, M22 and M13 with it on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/), with colour cameras and the [Optolong L-Pro](/equipment/optolong-l-pro-2-filter/) filter.
