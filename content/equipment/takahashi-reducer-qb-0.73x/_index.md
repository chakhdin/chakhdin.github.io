Takahashi's QB 0.73× reducer for the FSQ-85EDX brings the focal length down to 330 mm at f/3.9 and covers a 44 mm image circle, with 72.2 mm of back focus. The wider field fits large targets and the faster focal ratio shortens the exposures. I use it with the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) on the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/).
