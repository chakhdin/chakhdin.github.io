The ZWO ASI224MC is a colour planetary camera with the Sony IMX224 sensor: 1304 × 976 pixels (1.2 MP) of 3.75 µm, with read noise as low as 0.8 e⁻. It was my planetary camera until November 2024, mostly on the [Meade Maksutov 127/1900](/equipment/meade-maksutov-127/1900/) with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/), and once on [GAR's Merz-Dallmeyer 162mm](/equipment/gars-merz-dallmeyer-162mm/). Since then the planetary rig uses the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) and the monochrome [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/).
