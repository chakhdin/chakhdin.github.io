The Paramount ME is a robotic German equatorial mount made by Software Bisque in Golden, Colorado, USA, for permanent and remote observatories.

At Swan Reach, South Australia, it carries the [RCOS 16"](/equipment/rcos-16/) of the [Swan Reach remote rig](/rigs/swan-reach/), with an [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) guided through an off-axis guider.
