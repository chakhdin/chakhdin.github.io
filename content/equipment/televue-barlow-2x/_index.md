A TeleVue 2× Barlow lens, one of my oldest accessories. It doubles the effective focal length to reach the image scale needed for the planets: on the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) it gives about 4000 mm at f/20, in front of the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/) and the LRGB / IR-pass filter wheel.

Earlier planetary images used it on a Meade 127/1900 Maksutov (about 3800 mm) with the colour [ASI224MC](/equipment/asi224mc/).
