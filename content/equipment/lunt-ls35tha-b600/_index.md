The Lunt LS35THa is a dedicated H-alpha solar telescope with a 35 mm aperture and an internal etalon with a bandwidth under 0.75 Å, tuned by tilt. Mine was bought second-hand and came with a Lunt B600 blocking filter.

I use it on its own, and its etalon as the second filter of a double stack with the [Daystar Quark Chromosphere](/equipment/daystar-quark-chromosphere/) on the [Bresser 127L](/equipment/bresser-127l/), with the [ASI174MM](/equipment/asi174mm/).
