The ZWO ASI533MC Pro is a cooled colour camera with the square 1-inch Sony IMX533 sensor: 3008 × 3008 pixels (9 MP) of 3.76 µm. A two-stage thermoelectric cooler holds the sensor up to 35 °C below ambient. I used it on the [Skywatcher 150PDS](/equipment/skywatcher-150pds/) with the [Optolong L-Pro](/equipment/optolong-l-pro-2-filter/) filter for M22.
