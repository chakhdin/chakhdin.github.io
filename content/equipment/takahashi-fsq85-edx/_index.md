The Takahashi FSQ-85EDX is an 85 mm Petzval refractor with four lens elements, 450 mm focal length at f/5.3. It is one of my two main deep-sky telescopes, with the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) imaging train on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/).

I use it in two configurations:

- native, with the Takahashi 1.01× flattener (about 455 mm), which extends the corrected image circle to 44 mm;
- with the [Takahashi QB 0.73× reducer](/equipment/takahashi-reducer-qb-0.73x/), for about 330 mm at f/3.9 and a wider field.

Before the ASI2600MM Pro it was paired with the colour [ASI294MC Pro](/equipment/asi294mc-pro/).

It was a chance purchase: I found it as a good deal on AstroSell, complete with rings, a PrimaLuceLab Losmandy double plate and a Pegasus Astro FocusCube electronic focuser, so it was ready to use from the start. It is my second refractor and the first one that worked for me: with my earlier William Optics Megrez 72 and its original reducer I was never happy with the star shapes, especially at the edges of the field. I bought the QB 0.73× reducer later, again as a deal on AstroSell; with it the FSQ becomes a very fast wide-field instrument at f/3.9, well suited to the nebulae of our Galaxy.

I do not compensate the focus for temperature: N.I.N.A.'s autofocus refocuses as the stars' FWHM changes. Narrowband subs are almost always 600 s, occasionally 900 s; broadband subs, RGB and luminance, are 300 to 600 s. The sub lengths stay the same with the reducer.

It is a truly spectacular instrument: the stars are pinpoint across the whole width of the field. The ASI2600MM Pro is not a full-frame camera but has a slightly smaller APS-C sensor; even so, the stars stay excellent with the reducer too. This is one of the greatest satisfactions this refractor gives me.
