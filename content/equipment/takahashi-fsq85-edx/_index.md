The Takahashi FSQ-85EDX is an 85 mm Petzval refractor with four lens elements, 450 mm focal length at f/5.3. It is one of my two main deep-sky telescopes, with the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) imaging train on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/).

I use it in two configurations:

- native, with the Takahashi 1.01× flattener (about 455 mm), which extends the corrected image circle to 44 mm;
- with the [Takahashi QB 0.73× reducer](/equipment/takahashi-reducer-qb-0.73x/), for about 330 mm at f/3.9 and a wider field.

Before the ASI2600MM Pro it was paired with the colour [ASI294MC Pro](/equipment/asi294mc-pro/).
