The ZWO ASI174MM is an uncooled monochrome USB 3.0 camera with the Sony IMX174 global-shutter sensor: 1936 × 1216 pixels (2.3 MP) of 5.86 µm. It is my high-speed camera for lucky imaging, in two setups on the [NEQ6 Pro](/equipment/neq6-pro/):

- **Planetary:** on the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) with the [TeleVue 2× Barlow](/equipment/televue-barlow-2x/) and a ZWO EFW Mini 5 × 1.25" filter wheel holding [Optolong LRGB filters](/equipment/optolong-lrgb-filters/) and an IR-pass filter.
- **Solar:** on the [Bresser 127L](/equipment/bresser-127l/), in white light through a Tecnosky Herschel wedge, or in H-alpha with a [Daystar Quark Chromosphere](/equipment/daystar-quark-chromosphere/) double-stacked with the [etalon of a Lunt LS35](/equipment/lunt35-etalon-double-stack/).
