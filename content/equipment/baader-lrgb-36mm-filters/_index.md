Baader's LRGB set: a luminance filter for detail plus red, green and blue filters for colour, the standard way to take colour images with a monochrome camera. I use the 36 mm filters in the filter wheel of the [ASI2600MM Pro](/equipment/asi2600mm-pro/) on the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/), on their own or with the [Baader H-alpha filter](/equipment/baader-ha-7nm-36mm-filter/).
