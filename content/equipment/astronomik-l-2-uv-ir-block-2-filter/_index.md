The Astronomik L-2 is a luminance filter that passes visible light and blocks ultraviolet and infrared. Its passband lies between the wider L-1 and the narrower L-3: a compromise between throughput and the bloated stars that residual colour errors of the optics cause outside the visible band. I use the 2" (M48) version in front of the [ASI294MC Pro](/equipment/asi294mc-pro/), on the [Meade 8" ACF](/equipment/meade-8-acf/) and the [Takahashi FSQ85-EDX](/equipment/takahashi-fsq85-edx/).
