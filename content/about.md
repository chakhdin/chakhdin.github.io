I'm Sergey Shakhdinarov, an amateur astrophotographer living near Milan, Italy. I have been imaging the night sky since January 2022, and I'm a member of [Gruppo Astrofili Rozzano](https://www.astrofilirozzano.it), the amateur astronomy club based at the Civico Osservatorio Astronomico di Rozzano.

## Where I image

For deep-sky targets I drive to darker skies: Il Pratone in the Oltrepò Pavese and Saint Barthélemy in Valle d'Aosta. The Moon, the Sun and the planets are mostly shot from home in San Giuliano Milanese, under a light-polluted suburban sky.

Since October 2026 I also share a remote rig at [Swan Reach](/rigs/swan-reach/), in South Australia, with four friends: it opens up the southern sky. Those images are in the [Australia](/australia/) section.

## How

All my rigs in Italy ride on the same NEQ6 Pro mount; the setups are described in [My Rigs](/rigs/), and every image links to the equipment it was taken with.

- **Deep sky:** capture with NINA, processing in PixInsight with the RC Astro tools.
- **Planets, Moon and Sun:** capture with SharpCap, stacking with AutoStakkert! 4, solar deconvolution with ImPPG, mosaics stitched in Microsoft ICE, final processing in PixInsight.
