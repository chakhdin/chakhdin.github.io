Comet C/2025 A6 (Lemmon) with a vivid green coma and a long ion tail that shows fine filamentary structure, with the spiral galaxy NGC 3184 in the same field.

The Mount Lemmon Survey discovered the comet on 3 January 2025. It came closest to Earth on 21 October 2025 at 0.60 AU (89 million km), peaked around magnitude 3.5 a few days later and passed perihelion on 8 November at 0.53 AU (79 million km) from the Sun. NGC 3184 is a spiral galaxy in Ursa Major.
