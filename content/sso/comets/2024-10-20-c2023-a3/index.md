Comet C/2023 A3 (Tsuchinshan–ATLAS) a few days after its closest approach to Earth: a compact green coma, from the glow of diatomic carbon, trails a broad, bright dust tail across the frame.

Purple Mountain Observatory (Tsuchinshan) discovered the comet on 9 January 2023, and the ATLAS survey found it independently on 22 February. It passed perihelion on 27 September 2024 at 0.39 AU (58 million km) from the Sun, came closest to Earth on 12 October at 0.47 AU (71 million km) and peaked around magnitude −4.9 on 9 October.
