This capture used a very different instrument from the rest of the collection: a 162mm f/15 refractor built in 1875 by the Merz-Dallmeyer optical workshop, paired with a 2x Barlow lens. The long, slow optical path and 19th-century glass render Jupiter's belts with a distinct soft, warm character quite unlike the modern views elsewhere in this gallery, though the same equatorial banding and pale hollow near the South Equatorial Belt are still clearly resolved.
