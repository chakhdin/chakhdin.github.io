The International Space Station crosses the face of the Sun in a fraction of a second, so the transit is shown here as a sequence of frames merged into one composite, the station's path running diagonally across the disk past a few sunspots.

Captured as video panels, each stacked in AutoStakkert!3, sharpened in ImPPG and stitched into a mosaic.
