NGC 6781: The Snowglobe Nebula

"A stunning planetary nebula in Aquila, featuring a glowing blue core of ionized oxygen surrounded by a delicate red shell of hydrogen."

This image captures NGC 6781, widely known as the Snowglobe Nebula, a beautiful planetary nebula located in the constellation Aquila. The bubble-like structure is formed by the outer layers of a dying sun-like star being cast off into space. The intense ultraviolet radiation from the exposed white dwarf core illuminates the expanding gas, creating the distinct color separation seen here: a hot, glowing blue center dominated by ionized oxygen (OIII) and a cooler, delicate red outer shell composed primarily of hydrogen (H-alpha).

This image is a collaborative capture by Rino Morra and Sergey Shakhdinarov.
